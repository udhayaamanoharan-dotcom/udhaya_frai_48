# 400-word Reflection – complete in your own words

Write approximately 400 words covering:
1. The problem your project solves.
2. How the LLM extraction and Python validation work together.
3. One mistake or unexpected result you found during testing.
4. Your three red-team tests and what they taught you.
5. Why the faculty treasurer remains the human checkpoint.
6. What you learned about GitHub, testing, and responsible AI.

Do not submit this template unchanged. Describe what you personally built, tested, and understood.
